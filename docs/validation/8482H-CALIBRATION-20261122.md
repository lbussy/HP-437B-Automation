The middle table column is headed “EE%” above “dBm” and reads **20.00** for every row. Its meaning is not explained in the supplied record. It is not treated here as an operating limit or proof of linearity across power levels.

The chart lists these **typical measurement uncertainties of calibration-factor data**:

| Printed frequency band | Printed uncertainty |
| --- | --- |
| 100 kHz to 10 MHz | 0.87% |

The endpoints overlap as printed. No coverage factor or confidence level is specified in that table. These figures are not a complete uncertainty budget for a future meter, sensor, cable, attenuation, mismatch, and DUT measurement chain.

## Use and limits

The issuer reports Pass as a laboratory result. Before selected use, confirm the actual sensor label, connector, applicable manual, power limits, calibration method, and RF path. The chart's 20.00 column and frequency span do not establish safe input limits. Host/backend compatibility and absolute RF accuracy require separate project evidence.
