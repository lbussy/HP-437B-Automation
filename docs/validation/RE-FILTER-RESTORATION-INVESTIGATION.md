## Findings from the source and trace

The [filter text, printed 3-62/PDF 92](../../output/pdf/hp-437b-8482a/HP-437B-Operating-Manual-00437-90047.pdf#page=92), describes auto-filter enabling when display resolution is modified using the local RESOLN key. The [remote resolution procedure, printed 3-80/PDF 110](../../output/pdf/hp-437b-8482a/HP-437B-Operating-Manual-00437-90047.pdf#page=110), specifies RE plus resolution number and EN, without separately guaranteeing that filter-mode side effect. The old gate extended the local-key behavior to remote RE too strongly.

The actual FM4EN/RE1EN/TR1/read sequence returned SM manual-filter code 02. Its intermediate effective resolution was not independently read. That trace therefore does not distinguish a successfully applied RE1 that preserves manual filtering from a remote entry that did not take effect. SM has no resolution field, and an unchanged final RES2 cannot retrospectively prove the intermediate RE1 value. Fakes explicitly preserve this ambiguity rather than declaring the meter or encoder defective.

The restoration timeout occurred after accepted OC0/RE2EN/RA/FA writes, at the following TR1 read. The run lasted 22.711 seconds and failed before its 60-second work budget, so global budget exhaustion is not the observed cause. No raw reply was captured. The trace cannot identify whether the cause was source/range/filter transition timing, command-state interaction, or backend behavior.

[Triggering, printed 3-104/PDF 134](../../output/pdf/hp-437b-8482a/HP-437B-Operating-Manual-00437-90047.pdf#page=134), describes TR1 adding one filter point without clearing the filter, and TR3 continuously updating measurements. [Status, printed 3-33/PDF 63](../../output/pdf/hp-437b-8482a/HP-437B-Operating-Manual-00437-90047.pdf#page=63), updates SM after a measurement. These support testing continuous free-run refresh before the final status query, rather than forcing another immediate triggered acquisition after restoration changes. They do not guarantee a particular four-second latency or prove the original timeout's cause.
