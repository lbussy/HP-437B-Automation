Before the CL write, the assessment conservatively marks CAL and its reference output as potentially active, because even a partial write can take effect. ## Followup timeout and shutdown correction

The [first physical CAL](PI-CAL-20261114.md) completed, cleared its completion status, and verified POWER REF off/error-free, but its followup TR1 read hit the inherited two-second cap. The runner then skipped TR3/GTL and required manual local recovery. The exact physical cause of the delayed measurement is not established. The operating manual's [supplemental response characteristics, printed 1-10/PDF 20](../../output/pdf/hp-437b-8482a/HP-437B-Operating-Manual-00437-90047.pdf#page=20), allow response times above two seconds; its [trigger description, printed 3-104–105/PDF 134–135](../../output/pdf/hp-437b-8482a/HP-437B-Operating-Manual-00437-90047.pdf#page=134), says response depends on range/filter/resolution/sensor, TR1 ultimately holds the measurement, and TR3 restores free run. Those pages were reviewed alongside the retained trace. They justify a separate bounded CAL followup allowance; they do not prove why this run timed out or guarantee every configuration finishes in ten seconds.

The correction changes only the opt-in CAL path: one TR1 write, one read with up to ten seconds, then final status and normal TR3/GTL. It does not change filter, resolution, range, the accepted ZERO case, or any normal logging timeout. The read-specific allowance is removed in `finally`; subsequent commands/status reads retain two seconds. There is no continuation read, automatic read retry, or extra CAL.

If the CAL clear/new/post-clear sequence and reference-off status are known, the TR1 write completed, and its read was entered but failed, the worker now attempts **one disposal-only TR3 and one USB488 Go To Local**, then raw-closes the session. It never acknowledges or clears Instrument uncertainty and never queries or reuses that failed stream. TR3 intentionally abandons only the failed measurement after CAL is complete. A failed TR3 still permits one independent GTL attempt, with the free-run failure retained. An accepted shutdown does not promote the failed acquisition or overall case to success. Failed cleanup is explicitly recorded as such. Partial trigger writes, unknown CAL completion, cancellation/deadline gates, and pre-read budget rejection do not authorize this failed-read cleanup path.
